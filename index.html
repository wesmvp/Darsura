Rarity Guide

Each rarity has its own color and base strength range. Higher rarity cards usually have stronger passives, but some Common and Rare cards are still useful for farming.

Common / Gray White

Early-game cards. Good for learning the game, farming coins, or building starter strength.

Rare / Blue

Mid-tier cards with stronger economy, roll-control, and strength passives.

Epic / Purple

Strong cards that can shape your build with bigger rewards or risky tradeoffs.

God / Gold

Top-tier cards with the strongest scaling, jackpot effects, and late-game power.

How to Play

1 / Roll Cards

Spend 100 coins to roll one card or 500 coins to roll five cards. Cards are picked based on rarity odds.

2 / Keep or Trash

Keeping equips the card. Trashing uses the dust button and triggers your equipped card’s passive.

3 / Build Strength

Your strength is your main power stat. You gain it from trash effects, passives, and duplicate jackpot upgrades.

4 / Farm Coins

Coins let you roll more. Economy cards help you stay rolling longer and chase better cards.

Duplicate Upgrade Guide

A duplicate means rolling the exact same card you already have equipped. This is meant to be a rare jackpot path, not the main way to progress.

Main Systems

Dust Button / Trash

Dust is not a currency. It means pressing the trash/dust button on a rolled card to activate effects.

Hidden Passives

Some passives may show as ??? until revealed. Revealing costs 400 coins and saves permanently.

Overhead Display

Your equipped card appears above your character with its name, image, rarity color, and power.

Save System

The game saves coins, equipped card, strength, and revealed passives.

FAQ

Is dust a currency?
No. Dust just means trashing a rolled card with the dust button. The real saved values are coins, strength, equipped card, and revealed passives.
What is the strongest non-God card?
Rimuru is one of the strongest non-God strength farmers because +40 strength per trash is simple and consistent. Madara and Ryuk are very strong for coin farming.
Are duplicates supposed to be a good path?
No. Duplicates are supposed to be rare jackpot moments. Most players progress through trash effects, coin farming, roll control, and passives.
Why would I use coin farming cards?
More coins means more rolls. More rolls means more chances at stronger cards, better passives, and rare God pulls.
What should beginners focus on?
Beginners should focus on cards that give coins, refunds, or reliable strength. Nami, Chopper, Dimple, Naruto, and Rimuru are easy early-game options.

Progression Tips

Early Game

Use consistent cards. Dimple, Naruto, Nami, Chopper, and Rimuru are easy to understand and useful early.

Mid Game

Start choosing a build. Coin farming, strength farming, roll control, RNG burst, and jackpot duplicates all feel different.

Late Game

God cards define late game. Mob scales over time, Veldora has huge God duplicate potential, and Griffith rewards risky high-rarity trashing.

Smart Play

Do not switch cards randomly. Pick a card that supports what you are currently trying to do: farm coins, build strength, or hunt better cards.
