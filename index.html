Rarity Guide

Common / Gray White

Starter cards. Usually weaker, but some have useful early-game passives.

Rare / Blue

Better base strength and stronger utility passives for coin gain or faster growth.

Epic / Purple

High base strength cards. Great stepping stones toward late-game power.

God / Gold

Top-tier cards with the strongest base strength and best long-term scaling.

How to Play

Rolling

Spend coins to roll cards. A single roll costs 100 coins. Roll 5 costs 500 coins.

Keep

Keeping a card equips it. If it is the same card you already have equipped, it upgrades your strength.

Dust Button

Dust means trashing the rolled card. Dust is not a saved currency or value.

Reveal

Some passives are hidden as ???. Revealing costs 400 coins and saves permanently.

Progression Tips

Early Game

Use Common or Rare cards that help with coins or repeated trashing.

Mid Game

Start chasing Rare and Epic cards. Upgrade duplicates and reveal useful hidden passives.

Late Game

Focus on God cards and duplicate upgrades. God duplicates give the biggest strength jumps.

Save System

The game saves your coins, equipped card, equipped strength, and permanently revealed passives.

FAQ

Is dust a currency?
No. Dust is just the action of trashing a card with the dust button.
How do I get stronger?
Keep stronger cards, upgrade duplicates, and use passives that give strength when you trash cards.
What should I spend coins on?
Early on, use coins for rolls. Once you have a good card, revealing hidden passives becomes more useful.
